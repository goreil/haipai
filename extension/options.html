mjai → haipai uploader

Uploads the Mortal review from a mjai report page to your haipai instance.

No trailing slash needed. The bundled manifest only grants access to https://haipai.ylue.de/* — a different host also needs to be added to host_permissions in manifest.json.

The extension uploads as whoever is logged in to haipai in this browser, so there is nothing to connect and nothing to paste. Log in to haipai the way you normally do — password, Discord, or Google — and uploads just work. Log out of haipai and they stop.
